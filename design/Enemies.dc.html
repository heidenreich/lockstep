Know the line

Enemies & bosses

Blocked turns (3 = breaks step) Broken free, chasing you
The line

Drone

1 health · hits 1

Copies your every move. The backbone of the line.

Beat it: line it up with a pit and step that way.

Contrarian

1 health · hits 1

Moves the opposite way to you. Walks straight at you when you advance.

Beat it: step away from a pit to send it in.

Heavy

2 health · hits 2

Moves every other turn (zz means it rests next). Stops resting in a frenzy.

Beat it: let the line pass it, then pin it.

Elite

Runner

2 health · hits 1

Copies your move twice. Reaches pits, and you, before anyone else.

Beat it: give it a pit two tiles away.

Elite

Anchor

3 health · hits 1

Never moves; strikes anything beside it. A wall that fights back.

Beat it: clear the rest. A lone Anchor yields.

Bosses · pits deal 3 and they climb back out · all enrage at turn 30
Act 1 boss

The Colossus

8 health · hits 2 · moves every other turn

A giant Heavy that keeps step. A drone arrives every 6 turns.

Beat it: walk it into pits three times.

Act 2 boss

The Mirror

8 health · hits 1 · a Contrarian

Every 5 turns it flips the cadence: for 5 turns, everyone moves the other way.

Beat it: watch the countdown, plan both halves.

Act 3 boss

The Warden

9 health · hits 2 · never keeps step

Hunts you every other turn and steps around pits. Two drones arrive every 7 turns.

Beat it: a Trapdoor it can't see, or a Shove.
