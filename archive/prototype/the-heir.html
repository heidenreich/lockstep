The Heir

When you die, whatever killed you becomes you, and carries one of your skills. Build a line of murderers five generations long.

    Controls

    WASD or arrows to move or attack · Space wait · 123 skill, then a direction · Esc cancel · R restart

    How it works
    • Each turn you move one tile, wait, use a skill, or bump into an enemy to hit it. Then every enemy steps toward you and hits you if it ends next to you.
    • When you die, the enemy that struck the killing blow becomes your heir. It keeps its own body (health, damage, movement, native skill), starts where it stood, and inherits one skill from the character it killed. It leaves the wave; the wave goes on.
    • Dying costs you. The heir starts at half its health, or less if you'd already wounded it.
    • Each wave gives you 25 turns. After that, another enemy arrives every 10 turns until the wave is cleared.
    • Skills: press 1, 2 or 3, then a direction if the skill is aimed. Using one takes your turn; the number shows turns until it's ready. Enemies never use skills; theirs only wake up when they become you.
    • Knight 6 HP, hits 2, Cleave (hit all 4 sides, every 4 turns). You start as one; they never appear as enemies.
    • Goblin 3 HP, hits 1, Dash (up to 3 tiles in a line, every 3). Fast: every other turn it takes two steps. As your heir, every second action of yours is free: enemies don't respond.
    • Skeleton Archer 3 HP, hits 1, Bolt (2 damage to the first enemy in a line, every 3). Shoots along a clear row or column up to 4 tiles. As your heir, waiting shoots the nearest enemy that way.
    • Slime 4 HP, hits 1, Regrow (heal 2, every 6). Slow: acts every other turn. As your heir, enemies act twice after every second action of yours.
    • Orc 5 HP, hits 2, Charge (run in a line and hit what you reach for damage +1, every 4).
    • Wave n brings 2 + ⌊(n−1)/2⌋ enemies: goblins and slimes from wave 1, archers from wave 2, orcs from wave 3. The line ends when generation 5 dies. Score: 2 per wave cleared + 1 per kill.
