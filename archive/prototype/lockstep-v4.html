    How it works
    • Each turn you move one tile, wait, or bump into an enemy to hit it.
    • Lockstep: whichever way you press, every drone and heavy tries to move that way too. Contrarians move the opposite way. The room copies your input even when you attack or walk into a wall.
    • An enemy that moves into your tile hits you, and so does one that ends its move right beside you. Attacking, or stopping against a wall, lets whatever is lined up behind you crash into you.
    • Every wave after the first takes place in a different room: Crossroads, Gallery, Moat, Lanes or Funnel. Each moves the walls and pits, so the pushes that pay off change.
    • After each wave you pick one of three upgrades that bend the rules: deeper pits, shoving, crushing enemies against walls or each other, and more. They last the whole run.
    • Herd enemies into pits for 2 points each. Each pit holds 2 per wave, then fills in (dashed outline) until the next wave. Bump kills score 1, and clearing a wave scores twice its number.
    • An enemy blocked 3 turns in a row (by a wall, the edge or another enemy) breaks lockstep and chases you. A dot means it has been blocked; a red ring means it has broken free. A chaser that loses you (4 or more tiles away for 4 turns) falls back into step.
    • Waiting lets every enemy chase you for one turn and brings in a new one. While two or more enemies remain, another arrives every 8 turns, up to 2 extra per wave.
    • Each wave has 60 turns of patience. After that, the room goes into a frenzy: every enemy breaks step and chases you until the wave is cleared.
    • Heavies take 2 hits, hit for 2 and move every other turn. Everything else has 1 health and hits for 1.
