How it works
  • Each turn you move one tile, wait, or bump into an enemy to hit it.
  • The chamber is two rooms joined by one doorway. Each wave arrives in the room you're not in, so every enemy has to come through the door.
  • Your echo appears where you stood one delay ago and repeats your moves in order. The violet trail shows its next moves; 1 is where it goes next.
  • The echo bumps into whatever is in its way, and steps into the tile when it kills. A kill by your echo scores 3 and heals 1. Your own kills score 1.
  • Enemies chase you, hit you as soon as they reach you, and can't walk through the echo. Get your echo to stand in the doorway and it holds the door. Shades move every turn. Brutes take 2 hits, rest every other turn, and hit for 2.
  • Your echo has 3 health. Enemies next to it hit it when they can't reach you. If it's destroyed, it re-forms one delay later, back on your path.
  • With the hurt toggle on, if the echo bumps into you, you take 1 damage and it shoves past, swapping places with you.
