      How to play

      1. The room copies your every move. Step, and every enemy still in step moves the same way. Contrarians move the opposite way.
      2. Steer them into trouble. An enemy that steps into a pit is gone (each pit holds 2, then fills). Walls, the edge and other pieces stop them.
      3. Don't get stepped on. An enemy that moves into you, or lands beside you while in step, hits you. Bump an enemy to hit it for 1.
      4. Blocked 3 turns running, an enemy breaks free and chases you (red tally marks beside its head count the turns). Get 4 tiles away for 4 turns and it falls back into step.
      5. Cards break the rule: move without the room, move the room without you, and more. Each one goes on cooldown after use; Step is always free.
      6. Finish fast. At turn 30 a frenzy frees every enemy. Waiting is a move too, but every enemy chases and a new one arrives.
      The line
      Reading the board
      • Hover the board or the arrows to preview a move: chalk arrows show where everything goes, and the damage you'd take.
      • The chalk slate shows the turn, enemies left, the frenzy countdown and this room's bonus (extra gold if you carry it out).
      • Keys: WASD or arrows step, 1–4 arm a card, Space wait, Esc cancel. On a phone, tap a tile beside you.
