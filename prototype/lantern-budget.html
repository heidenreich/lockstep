How it works
  • Your light (0–12, start 10) is your health, your mana and how far you see. Hits, spells and the wick all spend it. At 0 the run ends.
  • Lit, you see a circle of radius ⌈light ÷ 2⌉ (Euclidean, tile centres). The wick burns 1 light every 6 lit turns.
  • Moths exist only in light. Inside your circle they hunt you and can be hit. Outside it, or while you're snuffed, they hang still and untouchable.
  • Shades exist only in darkness. They won't step into your light, but a shade pressed against its edge drinks 1 light every 3 turns. Inside your light they freeze and your hits pass through. Snuff the lantern and they come for you.
  • Everything you kill gives up its glow: moths +1 light, shades +2. Snuffing to hunt shades is how you earn light back.
  • F snuffs the lantern: no wick burn, no Bolt, you see only the 8 tiles around you. The dark is cold, though: −1 light every 12 snuffed turns. Relighting costs 1.
  • Flare and Bolt hurt both families. Flare works from the dark too: it relights the lantern as it bursts and catches whatever came close. Bumping is free. Each floor has stairs and 1–2 oil (+3 light, max 12). Score: 3 per floor descended, 1 per kill.
