Lockstep · side-view

The same rule, turned on its side. When you walk or jump, the whole line does too. Walk them off ledges, drop them into the abyss, land on their heads.

How it works
  • Each turn: walk left or right, jump, or wait. Walking into an enemy hits it.
  • Lockstep: every drone and heavy copies your move, jumps included. Contrarians walk the opposite way and never jump.
  • Gravity pulls everyone down one tile per turn. A jump lifts you two tiles and you hang there for that turn, so you can steer across a two-tile gap.
  • Enemies that fall off the bottom are gone (+2). Land on an enemy's head for 2 damage. Fall yourself and you lose 2 health and climb back where you last stood.
  • An enemy that walks into you, or lands on you, hits you. Blocked 3 turns in a row, it breaks step and chases you.
  • The exit opens once the stage is clear. After 50 turns on a stage, everything breaks step and more drop in.
